Gameplay Guide

Goal: Escape the dungeon. Clear the monsters. Save the townspeople.

How to Play
Use your trusty book of tactile maps to help you explore the grid-based dungeon. You begin each level in the top left corner of the map (A1).
Move
Use the Arrow Keys to move between grid cells, attack monsters, and collect items.
Interact
Interact with doors and shops by pressing Enter or Space bar.
Reveal the Map
Map spaces are hidden until you reach an adjacent square. After unlocking a door, all map spaces are revealed, and the location of any uncollected item(s) is announced.
Path Tones
Enable Path Tones in the Settings, and 4 tones will play to describe the path around you. Tones play in the following order: North, South, West, East (or Up, Down, Left, Right on the Arrow Keys). A static sound will play instead of a tone if the path is blocked in that direction.
Shortcut Keys
Use the W, A, S, D keys to quickly access information.
W
Open the settings menu.
A
Announce all available items on the map.
S
List player's current stats.
D
Describe current location.
Reset
Refresh the page to reset your game. You can use Ctrl + R, Cmd + R, or F5 to refresh.
Settings
Access the Settings menu from the home screen or by pressing W while playing. You can turn various audio features on and off and adjust your color scheme. When an audio feature is enabled, you can adjust its speed, pitch, and volume.
